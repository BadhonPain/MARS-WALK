ABOUT MARSWALK

The mission planning platform built for future Martian boots on the ground.

THE PROBLEM

Decades of robotic Mars exploration have generated petabytes of planetary data across separate instruments, orbiters, and research teams. But future human astronauts executing an extravehicular walk cannot consult disconnected scientific papers or static maps. They need real-time, consolidated spatial intelligence combining terrain steepness, suit consumable budgets, solar radiation, and scientific sample return value.

THE MARSWALK SOLUTION

MARSWALK fuses terrain, orbital imaging, environmental telemetry, and rover discoveries into one interactive tactical mission planner. It evaluates multiple paths using transparent, multi-factor scoring to recommend routes that minimize physical danger while maximizing scientific breakthrough.
