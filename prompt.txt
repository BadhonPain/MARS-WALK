Build a futuristic, hackathon-quality web application called:

"MARSWALK"
Tagline: "Plan the Walk. Discover the Unknown."

The core concept:
MARSWALK is an interactive Mars mission planning platform designed for future human explorers. It integrates multiple NASA Mars science and exploration datasets into one layered map so astronauts can select a destination, plan a Marswalk route, understand terrain and environmental conditions, identify scientific targets, and choose the safest and most scientifically valuable route.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT just a Mars information website.
It should feel like a real mission-control tool that could help an astronaut plan and execute a Marswalk.
